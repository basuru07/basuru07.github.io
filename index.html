01 · About

A bit about me

I was born in Colombo, Sri Lanka. I studied the Physical Science stream at Thurstan College, Colombo 7, and sat my GCE Advanced Level exams in 2020. In 2022 I joined General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University (KDU) for a BSc (Hons) in Computer Science.

At KDU I built my foundations in software development, cloud technologies and DevOps practice. Today I work as an Associate Systems Engineer, focused on keeping infrastructure dependable, automating the repetitive parts and shipping software that holds up in production.
